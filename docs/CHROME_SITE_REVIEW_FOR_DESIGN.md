## What to look for, page by page
1. **Purpose in 5 seconds:** can a newcomer tell what the page is for and what to do next? Is there one clear main action, or several competing ones?
2. **Duplication:** the same information or call to action repeated on one page or across pages; sections that say the same thing in different words; two ways to do one thing.
3. **Logic and order:** does the page follow the visitor's questions (what is it → is it for me → how much → when → how do I start)? Anything in the wrong place?
4. **Consistency:** prices, group sizes, schedules, start dates, lesson lengths, number of lessons, trial rules, and names (centre, teacher, course names) must match everywhere, in both languages. List every mismatch with both places.
5. **Completeness:** a question a visitor would have that no page answers; a dead end (a page with no next step); links that lead somewhere unexpected; English pages that send you to Romanian ones.
6. **Simplicity:** text that could be half as long; jargon (CEFR, A1/A2 without explanation); too many choices at once; walls of text on phones.
7. **Visual and mobile:** crowded areas, small text or tap targets, things covered by sticky bars, inconsistent spacing, cards or buttons in different styles for the same job, images that don't help.
8. **Trust:** what would make a cautious visitor hesitate (missing prices, vague terms, no real photos, claims without support)?
9. **Admin (if reviewed):** what takes too many taps for a daily task (book a lesson, record a payment, see who hasn't paid, see today's lessons); labels that are unclear; screens that are hard on a phone.

## Report format
1. **Top 10** improvements with the most impact for visitors, most important first, one line each plus the page(s).
2. **Per page:** a short table: issue · why it matters · suggestion (what to simplify, merge, move, remove or add) · severity (high/medium/low).
3. **Cross-site themes:** repeated problems (e.g. the same section style done three ways), with every place it occurs.
4. **Mismatches list:** fact · page A says · page B says.
5. **Questions for the owner:** things you could not judge without knowing the school's intent.
6. **Brief for Claude Design:** 5–8 concrete design tasks written so a designer can start, e.g. "Redesign /cursuri/grup/a1 so the first screen shows: what A1 is, the next group's date and free places, price, and one 'Înscrie-te' button; move the curriculum below." Keep the school's colours (green, cream, red) and fonts unless a change is the point of the task.
